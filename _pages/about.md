My fascination with tech began at a young age. Some of my earliest memories are taking apart the family PlayStation and practicing soldering on various electronics. Since then my passion for technology has never faded. In high school I worked in the repair center on Apple MacBooks. Later, I graduated from CSU Pueblo with a bachelor's in Computer Information Systems. I graduated with a Master's in Computer Information Systems from CSU Fort Collins in December 2024.

I have 10+ years of experience in IT, including 5 years in data science, 2 years in AI, and 3 years in cybersecurity. Additionally, I have many more years of experience working in various fields such as programming and electronics repair. I gained these skills through various volunteer and independent projects.

Every day, I strive to experience and learn more, with the hope that my skills can make a positive impact on the world.
